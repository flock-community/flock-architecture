# Event-driven

An event is a record of something that happened in the business. This deep dive is about building systems around those records: what it gives you, how far you can take it, and what it asks in return.

:::note

This deep dive is still being written. For now, the [overview](/) explains the idea in short.

:::

## What this deep dive will answer

- What is the difference between storing the current state and recording what happened?
- What makes a good event, and why does its name matter so much?
- How does keeping events give you the full history of your business?
- What is the difference between passing events along and rebuilding your data from them?
- What does it ask of a team, and when is a classic request-and-response system the right choice?
