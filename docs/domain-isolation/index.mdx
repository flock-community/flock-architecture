# Domain isolation

The domain is the part of the software that captures how the business works. This deep dive is about keeping it on its own: what that means, how you find the right boundaries, and how you guard them over time.

:::note

This deep dive is still being written. For now, the [overview](/) explains the idea in short.

:::

## What this deep dive will answer

- What is a domain, and why should everything depend on it while it depends on nothing?
- How do you make sure engineers and the business use the same words for the same things?
- How do you find the boundaries between the parts of a business, and decide who owns what?
- Why do parts refer to each other by an identifier and nothing more?
- When is it fine to skip all of this?
